# Discover

Find the real job before choosing a solution.

## Goal

Move from a loose market or user observation to a defensible problem statement
and opportunity map grounded in direct evidence.

## Flow

1. Identify the user, buyer, trigger, and context.
2. Research direct language and observable behavior: questions, complaints,
   workarounds, purchases, switching, and repeated tasks.
3. Separate observations from inferred jobs, desired outcomes, quality criteria,
   and feelings.
4. Map the current solution landscape, including doing nothing and manual
   workarounds.
5. Connect existing features and behaviors back to the jobs they serve.
6. Use Evidence to distinguish well-supported needs from attractive stories.
7. End with a small set of opportunity hypotheses, not a product specification.

## Useful output

```text
WHO needs to DO WHAT, under WHAT CONDITIONS, so they can ACHIEVE WHAT?
```

A feature with no job attached is suspicious. A claimed job with no behavioral
or source evidence should remain an assumption.
