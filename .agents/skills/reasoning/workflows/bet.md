# Bet

Generate a small number of genuinely different bets before converging.

## Goal

Prevent premature commitment to the first plausible solution by forcing
competing product, technical, or strategic hypotheses to become explicit.

## Flow

1. Start from the evidence and opportunity statement, not from a favored
   implementation.
2. Generate roughly three materially different bets.
3. For each bet, state target user or system, job, expected outcome, table-stakes
   requirements, intended performance wedge, differentiator, and major
   assumptions.
4. Identify what evidence would distinguish the bets from one another.
5. Eliminate bets that are dominated or contradict existing evidence.
6. Prototype only where concreteness is needed to compare them.
7. Use Experiment when reality can cheaply discriminate among the remaining
   bets.

Variations of one favorite idea do not count as different bets.
